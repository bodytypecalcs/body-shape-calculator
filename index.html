    Step one

    How to measure yourself correctly

    Accurate numbers make the difference between two neighbouring shapes. Use a soft tape measure, stand relaxed in front of a mirror and wear light, close-fitting clothes or underwear.

    1

    Bust

    Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, keeping it parallel to the floor. Wear a non-padded bra. Men measure the shoulders at their widest point instead.

    2

    Waist

    Find your natural waist – the narrowest point of your torso, usually just above the belly button. Breathe out normally and do not pull the tape tight.

    3

    High hip

    Measure around the top of your hip bones, roughly 7 inches (18 cm) below your waist. This number separates the spoon shape from the bottom hourglass.

    4

    Hips

    Stand with feet together and measure around the widest part of your hips and seat. Check in the mirror that the tape stays level all the way round.

    The shapes

    Female body shapes explained

    The calculator sorts your proportions into the following shapes. Most people sit close to a border between two shapes, which is why the result also shows how near you are to the alternatives.

    Hourglass

    Bust and hips are almost the same size with a clearly defined waist – the classic balanced curve.

    Top Hourglass

    A defined waist with a bust that is slightly fuller than the hips.

    Bottom Hourglass

    A defined waist with hips slightly fuller than the bust.

    Spoon

    Hips noticeably wider than the bust, with a “shelf” at the high hip that gives a rounded lower half.

    Triangle (Pear)

    Hips wider than the bust, with a softer, less pronounced waist.

    Inverted Triangle

    Shoulders and bust are broader than the hips, with a straighter lower body.

    Rectangle

    Bust, waist and hips are fairly similar for a straight, athletic silhouette.

    Apple

    The midsection is the fullest area, often with slimmer legs and arms.

    Male body types

    Trapezoid

    Broad shoulders and chest tapering to a medium waist – the most balanced male frame.

    Inverted Triangle

    Very broad shoulders and a narrow waist, typical of athletic upper bodies.

    Rectangle

    Shoulders, waist and hips are similar in width.

    Triangle

    Hips are wider than the shoulders, with a fuller lower body.

    Oval

    The waist is the widest part of the body, often with a rounder midsection.

    Behind the numbers

    How the body shape calculator works

    The female results use the logic of FFIT – the Female Figure Identification Technique developed by researchers at North Carolina State University for the apparel industry. Instead of looking at single measurements, FFIT compares the differences between bust, waist, high hip and hips.

    The rules are checked in a fixed order and the first rule that fits decides the shape. Centimetres are converted to inches first, because the original thresholds were defined in inches. The male mode uses the ratio of shoulders to waist and the comparison of shoulders and hips.

    Hourglass

    Bust ≈ hips (within 1–3.6 in) and waist at least 9–10 in smaller

    Spoon

    Hips more than 2 in larger than bust, high hip ≥ 1.193 × waist

    Triangle

    Hips at least 3.6 in larger than bust, waist difference under 9 in

    Inverted triangle

    Bust at least 3.6 in larger than hips, waist difference under 9 in

    Rectangle

    Bust and hips within 3.6 in, waist less than 9 in smaller

    Bonus value

    Waist-to-hip ratio (WHR)

    Your waist-to-hip ratio is your waist measurement divided by your hip measurement. Besides describing your silhouette, it is used by the World Health Organization as a simple indicator of where the body stores fat.

    A waist of 28 in and hips of 38 in give a ratio of 28 ÷ 38 = 0.74. The value is a rough screening number only and says nothing about fitness, beauty or overall health on its own.

    FAQ

    Body shape questions, answered

    How do I find out my body shape?

    Measure your bust, waist, high hip and hips with a soft tape measure, then enter the numbers into a body shape calculator. It compares the differences between your measurements and returns the shape that matches your proportions.

    What is the most common female body shape?

    In the research behind the FFIT method, the rectangle shape was the most common among the women measured, followed by the spoon and bottom hourglass shapes. A true hourglass is less common than many people think.

    Should I measure in inches or centimetres?

    Either works. The calculator converts centimetres to inches internally because the shape rules were developed using inch-based differences.

    Can my body shape change?

    Your proportions can shift with age, pregnancy, hormones, training or weight changes, so your result can change over time. Your skeletal frame, such as shoulder and hip width, stays largely the same.

    Is waist-to-hip ratio a health measure?

    The World Health Organization uses waist-to-hip ratio as one indicator of abdominal fat. It is only a rough screening value and does not replace advice from a doctor.

    Does the calculator work for men?

    Yes. Switch to the male mode and enter your shoulder circumference, waist and hips. The calculator returns one of five male body types: trapezoid, inverted triangle, rectangle, triangle or oval.
